## Both suprema and strict nonattainment

`PrescribedExtinctionPair` (`PrescribedExtinctionClass.lean:9–15`) is a dependent record of one actual admissible `TwoLayerBody`, its two weak outgoing Maxwell states, and the two original optical-work extinctions equal to the positive target. Such equalities legitimately define the fixed-extinction class; they do not appear as premises of the theorem constructing a member.

The normalized supremum set is the range of `s.asymmetry`, and the dimensional supremum set is the range of `|A_+−A_-|` (`PrescribedExtinctionClass.lean:17–27`). The dimensional quantity is proved `target * asymmetry` using the nonzero target (`29–34`). Existence theorem `theorem11` supplies a member for every `0<ε<1`, yielding actual nonempty sets and elements above each candidate smaller upper bound (`36–95`).

`FullPrescribedSuprema.lean:8–31` proves **for every member** `|A_+−A_-|<target` and `Γ<1`, using positive absorption in each incidence at positive extinction and the proved passive upper bounds. This is a legitimate alternate strictness proof within this strictly lossy class; it does not restrict to near-optimal bodies. Lines `33–59` prove both least upper bounds and `sSup` equalities, explicitly passing the set-nonemptiness proofs. Lines `61–75` prove neither extremal value belongs to the corresponding set. The final aggregate includes these claims at `PhysicalTheorem11.lean:15–29`.

Thus the two asserted physical suprema are separately represented: `sup Γ=1` is dimensionless, and `sup |A_+−A_-|=E*` has units of area. No empty-set, unbounded-set or zero-denominator convention supplies either result.

### C2 — avoid claiming that every displayed ordinary intermediate formula is separately formalized

- **Location:** manuscript `main.tex:139–155` (`green-bound`), `298–355` (`weyl`–`current-error`), `361–378` (`angle-bracket`–`root-scaling`); actual alternatives `CompactOutgoingOperator.lean:206–330`, `SlabConvolution.lean:63–72`, `UniformBodyResponse.lean:9–31`, `PhysicalRankOneExtinctionBracket.lean:27–106`.
- **Severity:** Informational correspondence limitation; not a target gap.
- **Finding:** The Lean argument proves sufficient operator boundedness with a different finite constant, proves localization in physical space, and obtains exact extinction from a different bracket. Inspection does not establish that Lean separately certifies the manuscript's exact Schur constant, its Weyl split, its particular `R^−1` endpoints, or its root-scaling assertion. The manuscript already acknowledges different proof organization and excludes certification of every intermediate exposition (`main.tex:434,440`).
- **Repair:** Preserve that limitation in future descriptions and correspondence tables. If a claim of equation-by-equation certification is desired, add separate declarations for those quantitative intermediate statements. No weakening or modification of the target is needed.

### C3 — the canonical exterior representative is essential

- **Location:** `ScatteringStatePhysicalRadiation.lean:11–22,49–55`; `FullPhysicalOpticalTheorem.lean:15–26`; manuscript `main.tex:80,432,471`.
- **Severity:** Informational mathematical qualification, correctly handled in the current manuscript.
- **Finding:** Physical flux is computed on the reconstructed outgoing representative, whose fields agree with the weak state almost everywhere. It is not a claim about arbitrary pointwise values of a raw weak state on each sphere. A countable union of spheres has volume zero; raw weak fields can be modified there without changing weak equations, while their surface integrals can change. This is a genuine reason to keep the representative convention, not a defect in the target as stated.
- **Repair:** Retain the explicit canonical-representative wording whenever quoting the formal physical-flux conclusion. A stronger raw-pointwise flux assertion would require additional representative regularity and is unnecessary for this theorem.
