The bibliography contains 17 sources. All are primary publications, author preprints, or an official conference program. Bibliographic fields were taken from the supplied manuscripts and checked against accessible publisher/author/institutional records where available. No quotation is fabricated and no primary priority conclusion is inferred from a failed search.

Retained/expanded primary-paper references:
- Miller and Monticone (2026), arXiv:2605.24738v1, Section 4.1: precise OQ7 context; not all versions of OQ7 are claimed solved.
- Sounas and Alu (2014), doi:10.1364/OL.39.004053: publisher abstract confirms extinction equality and asymmetric scattering in the presence of absorption. Full equation comparison remains for expert review.
- Hopkins et al. (2015), doi:10.3390/photonics2020745: publisher/institutional primary records confirm modal directional absorption/scattering at conserved extinction; constituent reciprocal materials are not relabeled nonreciprocal.
- Schab et al., including Gustafsson (2020), doi:10.1364/OE.410520: prescribed-loss/material absorption-scattering multiobjective tradeoffs; different fixed-resource quantifiers.
- Kononchuk et al. (2019), doi:10.1103/PhysRevApplied.12.054046: complete layered-sheet Faraday isolator is magnetically nonreciprocal. The reciprocal polarizer component is referenced as a selective-attenuation precedent based on the supplied manuscript, not as an equivalent compact theorem.
- Manez-Espina et al. (2026), doi:10.1002/nap2.70145: publisher and author-institution records verify TCMT directional-absorptance optimization and full-wave comparisons. Model and observable differ from dimensional compact prescribed-extinction supremum.

Added or restored from supporting literature relative to the primary bibliography:
- Armitage (2014), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.90.035135, arXiv:1311.5556: established Jones-matrix/polarization mechanism.
- Ra'di, Asadchy, Tretyakov (2013), doi:10.1109/TAP.2013.2271892, arXiv:1211.3549: thin absorbers and opposite-side sheet responses. Reciprocal and nonreciprocal material subclasses must be distinguished.

- Bruno, Lyon, Perez-Arancibia, Turc (2016), doi:10.1137/15M1033782, arXiv:1507.04445: windowed Green methods for layered backgrounds; not literal sharp material truncation with the present normalized angle quantifiers.

- Mann and Alu (2023), official Metamaterials program: directly announced maximal directional dipolar absorption. Program, date, authors, and abstract are verified. Full equations/normalization and equivalence remain unverified. No invented DOI or journal citation was added.
