# Source-verified comparison matrix

Read with TARGETED_PRIORITY_REPORT.md and SOURCE_REGISTRY.md. The nine requested criteria are split across three tables for readability. IDs refer to the registry's primary sources and locators.

**Evidence labels:** F = full primary paper/accepted manuscript/preprint inspected; A = official abstract; P = publisher introduction/section previews; H = full publisher prose with mathematical rendering incomplete; M = institutional bibliographic record only. F does not imply access to every separately published supplement. “Not established” means that the inspected result supplies no verified implication with the target quantifiers. For A/P/H/M, unknown details remain unknown.

The benchmark is the same unchanged body, the same real y-polarized unit plane wave reversed from +z to −z, physical cross sections, arbitrary fixed k>0, P>1, Q>0 and exactly E*>0, with total sphere radiation and vanishing *material-axis* coupling.
