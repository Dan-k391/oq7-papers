# Quartic Maxwell Lean formalization

This is a new, separate formalization of results used in the quartic Maxwell manuscript. It uses Lean 4.34.1 and Mathlib commit `d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612`, matching the unchanged prescribed-extinction project. Historical manuscripts, arithmetic certificates and physical proofs remain preserved in their original directories.

The implemented proofs include the actual support-masked Maxwell operator estimates `‖K₀‖ ≤ 1` and `‖Kₖ−K₀‖ ≤ 3k²aᵥ²/4` for bounded measurable supports. They derive the sharp dyadic Green bound, exact Newton rearrangement and vector L² Schur estimate, and identify the operator with the outgoing-volume distribution. The complete vector radiation map is constructed on the same support and its squared norm is proved at most `8πV/3` using the actual sphere area measure and second moments.

The completed upper-bound checkpoint also constructs actual measurable tensor multipliers, their inverses with the factor `m/M²`, and the single support-restricted volume inverse. It proves its equivalence to every locally finite-energy outgoing weak Maxwell state, the literal material absorption and extinction integrals, complete far-field power, power balance, reciprocal positive extinction, and both exact bounds in original Theorem 1.1. These are actual physical correspondences for the stated tensor class; the final result does not assume an inverse or a desired operator bound as model data.

Two earlier preserved checkpoints passed compilation, exhaustive public declaration type/axiom inventories and fresh replay through Lean's own kernel checker. `verification/checkpoints/upper-2026-10-14/` contains the 26-module, 300-declaration proof of original Theorem 1.1. `verification/checkpoints/upper-fixed-pattern-2026-10-14/` contains all 39 ingredient modules, 487 public declarations and the full original Theorem 4.1, including actual fixed-wavenumber shrinking-body dilation. Both records bind exact proof-source hashes; the latter also preserves copies of every checked source. Fresh replay uses Lean's own kernel implementation, rather than an independently implemented kernel.

The final checkpoint verifies all eight original numbered theorem and lemma cores: Theorems 1.1–1.3 and 4.1, and Lemmas 3.1–3.4. All 76 ingredient modules and their aggregate compile; every one of the 1,048 explicit public theorem/lemma/definition/abbreviation exports has its exact type and standard-axiom inventory checked; fresh kernel replay succeeds. The verifier also checks 128 required public roots and pins the full types of 20 physical roots covering all eight original claims. Twelve isolated rejection controls pass, including weakened-physical-theorem and changed-remainder-constant controls. Exact checked-source records and source copies are preserved at `verification/checkpoints/all-numbered-theorems-2026-10-15/`.

The finite-sphere results use actual continuum inverses. The heterogeneous theorem has the original exact coefficient interval, `21t⁶/2000` remainder for `t≤1/4`, and `27t⁴/1299125` lower bound for `t≤1/40`. The homogeneous theorem quantifies positive constants before all real transverse unit illumination pairs: its constants depend only on the fixed resources, the material is exactly `χ=imI`, and the relative sphere radius is fixed before shrinking. The original quantitative homogeneous relative-error certificate and coefficient interval are also derived. Signed quantities are explicitly matched to the manuscript's `exp(+ikn·x)` incident labels.

See [CLAIM_STATUS.md](CLAIM_STATUS.md) and the 92-entry machine-readable `verification/coverage.json` for the original numbered theorem/lemma, equation and other substantive claim scopes. Completion of the numbered theorem cores is distinguished from the whole original paper: auxiliary displays, empirical numerical tables, bibliographic positioning and human review have separate entries and are not inferred from a successful Lean build.

Run `python verify.py --lean-bin PATH_TO_PINNED_LEAN_BIN` after restoring the prescribed-extinction project's pinned dependencies and Mathlib build cache. It compiles every registered new source, rejects missing modules and proof escapes, checks exhaustive declaration types and axiom inventories, compares the complete physical core theorem types with their pinned manifest, hashes the checked files and records the outcome in `verification/report.json`. `--fresh-kernel` additionally replays the aggregate import closure through Lean's own kernel checker; that is not an independently implemented kernel. Separate negative controls test proof-escape, false-proof, untrusted-axiom and module-census rejection without modifying the checked project.

The verifier does not modify papers. A manuscript should describe only the actual verification scope after the corresponding physical theorem is closed and its source hashes are recorded.
