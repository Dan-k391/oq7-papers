This revision changes only the new generalized-paper proof project and its
verification documentation. Historical proof libraries, original manuscripts,
archives and pinned Lean/Mathlib revisions remain unchanged.

  equations, physical vacuum admittance basis, passivity and reciprocity.

  relevant block, all incoming/outgoing boundary states and continuity.
- Connected the oblique family at normal incidence to the earlier actual
  absorbing-slab/lossless-plate construction and its exact extinction powers.

  continuity in the tunable plate parameter and exact extinction tuning.

  incoming/outgoing vacuum radiation expansions.

  proved total-boundary interference cancellation, right-port orientation and
  net absorption equal to incoming minus outgoing power.
- Assembled original Corollary 6.5 under fixed P/Q material caps with exact common

  relative-one approach. Recorded actual three-dimensional tensor caps.
