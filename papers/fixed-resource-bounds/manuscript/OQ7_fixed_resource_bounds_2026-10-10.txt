Abstract
We establish a strictly positive resource-dependent gap below unit extinction-normalized
directional absorption asymmetry for arbitrary compact reciprocal dielectric bodies in vacuum.
The sufficient material resource is the integrated loss-weighted responseM =

prior art. The optimal fixed-resource Maxwell supremum, simultaneous attainability by one local
material object, and the class with only a one-sided real permittivity cap remain unresolved.
These distinctions prevent a claim that the original Open Question 7 is fully closed.

7.4 Removing a full tensor loss floor
The integrated condition(12) permits nonuniform and vanishing loss when responding directions
remain controlled. It excludes a lossless direction with nonzero susceptibility. Positive trace loss,
positive total integrated loss, or positivity only on one polarization does not imply that condition.
The earlier planar vector construction exploits precisely such polarization structure; its scalar
determinant lower bound cannot be transferred to general tensors.
If one merely fixes size and bounded positive real contrast while allowing arbitrary vanishing loss,
the current proof need not have a boundedM. A replacement argument based on compactness would
need uniform Maxwell resolvent estimates, a specified topology for the coefficients and geometry,
a justified homogenization closure, and uniqueness for the limiting class. Weak convergence of
coefficients alone supplies none of these automatically. Uniqueness results for piecewise regular
anisotropic coefficients [21] should not be cited as a theorem for an arbitrary enlarged essentially
bounded class. We leave that sharper loss-free-resource question unresolved.
7.5 Multiple channels, other backgrounds, and bandwidth
The radiation-efficiency inequality is independent of the incident plane wave once its induced current
is given. For a general incident electric fieldf, the extinction estimate uses

In a waveguide, a substrate, or another background, the complete positive radiation operator
includes every open and guided channel. A bound on that operator and a compatible loss metric

automatically valid. Uncounted outgoing channels cannot be called absorption.

corresponding pointwise bounds, but a genuinely sharper bandwidth tradeoff requires constraints
on dispersion, oscillator strength, static response, or other causal resources. No finite-bandwidth
optimum is proved here.

The fixed-resource existence question is answered affirmatively for a broad, physically explicit
class, without a minimum feature size or multipole cutoff. An arbitrarily small deficit1−Γ forces
divergence of the integrated response and of the product¯ηkaunder the derived necessary-cost
inequalities. At positive extinction the exact radiation resolvent yields an additional obstruction.
These statements make the resource-free supremum compatible with a quantitative physical cost.
They do not identify the sharp resource-constrained supremum. The main next mathematical
task is a joint optimization for the two currents that enforces the same local reciprocal constitutive
operator, including the reactive Maxwell constraints, followed by a physical construction or a proof
of a residual gap. Cross-sourceT-operator methods provide a concrete prior framework for that
step [15, 16]. The absence of a lower real-part bound is a separate theorem-or- counterexample
problem and should not be silently folded into the proved capped class. The original OQ7 is thus
substantially narrowed, but not fully closed across its resource, constitutive, normalization, and
bandwidth interpretations.
