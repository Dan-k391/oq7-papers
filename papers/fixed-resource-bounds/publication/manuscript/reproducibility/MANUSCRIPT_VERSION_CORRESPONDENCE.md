# Manuscript version correspondence

`publication/manuscript/main.tex`, the bibliography and PDF are exact copies of the current `arxiv/` versions identified in the package manifest. Historical originals remain under `manuscript/` at the paper root. Original audit paths are unchanged. The copied final proof records, where supplied, retain their original reading-version hashes; the separate manuscript binding records subsequent editorial revisions. No historic report is rewritten to claim it checked a later manuscript.
