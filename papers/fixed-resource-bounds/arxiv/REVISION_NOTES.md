## Corrections and clarifications

- Typeset the diameter-to-enclosing-radius constant unambiguously as `sqrt(3/8)`, as required by `2a^2 <= 3R^2/4`. It is not `sqrt(3)/8`. The isodiametric volume and enclosing-radius estimates remain distinct.
- Restored conjugation bars lost by text extraction: the opposite electric incident vector is the conjugate of the first, the magnetic field acquires a minus sign under time reversal, and the sphere compares `L e` against `L conjugate(e)`. The spectral addition theorem applies to complex transverse unit polarization.
- Stated the angular-wave curl relation only up to its immaterial unit phase; the positive singular-value/eigenvalue calculation and its constants are unchanged.
- Preserved the integrated response budget's volume units and the dimensionless radiation eigenvalues. The current optimizer includes the factor `i t / D_alpha`; the feasible-target endpoint is `t = D_w`.
- Explicitly distinguished the general complex-polarization cubic example from the later opposite real-linear quartic refinement. The older paper's statement that real-linear matching remains wholly open is obsolete in this collection and was replaced with the precise companion scope. No new quartic proof was imported into this paper.
- Used the complete-Maxwell remainder argument rather than describing the sphere as a physical dipole-only model. Its coefficient is `160/2071`; exact intermediate values are work `931950/4118941`, norm difference `324000/4118941`, and their ratio `720/2071`.
- Retained the separation between a mathematically exact one-current relaxation and a physically attainable two-current fixed-resource supremum. Independent relaxed currents are not asserted to be generated by the same constitutive object.

## Omitted material and limitations

The historical interval-certificate table and retarded discrete-dipole table were removed. The described scripts, data files, directed-rounding environment and outputs were not supplied as a current verified numerical release; this revision does not promise their replay or repeat unsupported numerical residual claims. The analytical tail bounds and all exact coefficients remain, so no theorem depends on those tables. The audit of earlier compact/generalized manuscripts, historical Lean correspondence corrections, adversarial-review language and reproduction appendix were also removed from the scientific article. Their original context remains in the preserved PDF.

The response/efficiency gap is sufficient, not proved optimal. The radius operator's large-size order is not a physical asymmetry construction. The circular sphere does not establish isotropic sharpness or fixed-positive-extinction sharpness. The general rough six-component extension assumes well-posedness. One-sided real caps, uncontrolled responding lossless sectors, other backgrounds and optimal bandwidth tradeoffs require additional arguments. No Lean code was read, edited or executed, and no independent human peer-review claim is made. Build and rendered-PDF checks belong to the release validation record prepared separately.
