BOUNDARY CASE E_min = E_* -- RESEARCH STATUS
Date: 17 October 2026

Result: constructive candidate analytical proof, in the unrestricted two-layer
reciprocal tensor class of the cited manuscript and earlier fixed-band note.
For any finite positive-frequency band B, interior center omega_c, material
caps P > 1 and Q > 0, target E_* > 0, and epsilon > 0, the proposed construction
gives one finite body with fixed passive causal finite Lorentz laws satisfying:
  X(omega_c) = E_*;
  X(omega) > E_* at every other frequency in B;
  inf_B Gamma > 1 - epsilon.
Consequently the proposed boundary-class worst-case supremum is one and is
not attained. The spectrum is not exactly flat.

Order of construction:
1. Shape and align a minimum of a fixed damped planar reference spectrum.
2. Establish a nonzero passive material direction and a negative Fano lobe.
3. Choose finite aperture and small strictly positive isotropic loss.
4. Only then narrow a positive-residue Lorentz resonance.
5. Tune its center to stationarity and use coupling amplitude to hit E_*.
The proof uses material-parameter Cauchy estimates, not frequency-derivative
convergence as the aperture grows. The global minimum is treated with separate
inner curvature/gap estimates and an outer tail estimate.

Dependencies and limitations:
This draft inherits the original manuscript's weak-Maxwell/operator framework
and the earlier draft's C0 frequency-uniform aperture theorem. It is not a new
Lean verification, formal certification, external human review, or novelty
certification. Three GPT-6.1-sol subagents reviewed the construction; their
identified technical corrections were incorporated. No remaining substantive
blocker was identified in those AI reviews, which are not independent human
peer review. Caps apply on B. No common geometry/resource bound, linewidth
floor, or positive material-loss floor is imposed. Fixed-resource bounds and
restricted isotropic classes are not resolved by this note.

A separate proposition excludes exactly constant positive extinction on an
open interval for the finite positively damped Lorentz subclass specified in
the note. It is not stated for arbitrary passive constitutive laws.

Numerical diagnostics:
boundary_checks.py checks an explicit analytic surrogate of the tuning lemma,
not a Maxwell/device simulation. All twelve recorded consistency checks pass.
The surrogate is not asserted to obey all Maxwell realizability constraints;
only the added scalar Lorentz oscillator is checked for positive loss.
No asymmetry ratio, material caps, or device dimensions are computed. Dense
sampling is not a continuum-band certificate or a proof of the theorem.
The broadest resonance tested fails the sampled global-minimum check despite
center stationarity; narrower resonances pass, illustrating the order of limits.
