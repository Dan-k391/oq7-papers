Scope and further research

From one frequency to a prescribed band

The prescribed-extinction paper establishes a unit supremum of normalized absorption asymmetry at a single frequency, with exactly prescribed positive extinction. It imposes upper material caps but no common bound on device size or volume. Its causal material realization matches the selected frequency.

The proposed broadband extension asks for one device, with fixed geometry and passive causal material dispersion, to maintain high contrast and a positive extinction floor throughout a band chosen in advance. Two questions remain: whether the unit supremum persists with unrestricted resources, and what sharp contrast–bandwidth tradeoff holds with fixed geometric and material resources, beyond the existing monochromatic resource bounds. These are research targets; no broadband theorem or verification is claimed.
