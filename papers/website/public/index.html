Broadband candidate proof · 17 October 2026

A candidate proof on a prescribed band

The single-frequency theorem now has a separate analytical candidate extension: on any fixed finite positive-frequency band B, with a chosen interior center, one fixed finite passive causal two-layer reciprocal tensor body has X(center) = E* = min_B X, with strict inequality elsewhere, while inf_B Γ is arbitrarily close to 1. The candidate supremum is one and is not attained; the extinction spectrum is not exactly flat.

This is a CANDIDATE proof, with no new formal verification or independent human review. It inherits the earlier weak-Maxwell/operator framework and the strict-floor note’s C0 frequency-uniform aperture theorem. Material caps apply bandwise; the construction imposes no common geometry or resource bound, resonance-linewidth floor, or positive material-loss floor. Fixed-resource optima and an all-isotropic construction remain open.

The boundary note extends the earlier strict-floor candidate (0 < E_min < E*). Their numerical checks are limited diagnostics, not continuum-band certificates or finite-device simulations. The original research status and verification records are preserved in the source downloads.
