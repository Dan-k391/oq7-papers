## 4. Does Paper B rigorously prove sup Γ=1 for actual compact 3D Maxwell scatterers?

**Yes by the ordinary proof, with the stated resource-unrestricted class. ORDINARY PROOF ONLY.** For each k>0,P>1,Q>0, the reflectionless tensor plate works with an integer index choice respecting P. A finite weakly mismatched, sufficiently attenuating absorber gives planar Γ close to one. For that fixed finite stack, adding a sufficiently small fixed `iδI` produces strictly positive loss while respecting Q and preserving the ratio by transfer continuity. This is the compact-object statement, not merely an infinite slab or finite-power beam. It imposes no common diameter, volume, thickness, integrated loss, lower loss, feature size or bandwidth. Passivity gives Γ≤1; the complete optical theorem prevents equality for an individual regular compact object with positive extinction. The dipole family's extinction tending to zero is not a universal claim about this large-body sequence.
